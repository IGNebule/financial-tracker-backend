# ADR-NNNN: Short title in sentence case

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** (who was involved)

## Context

What is the problem, constraint, or opportunity? Keep this factual. Include
the forces at play: technical, organizational, time, cost.

## Decision

What did we choose? State it as a declarative sentence in the present
tense: "We will ...". Include enough detail that a future reader does not
need to consult the code to understand the shape of the decision.

## Consequences

### Positive

- What gets easier or better because of this decision?

### Negative

- What gets harder, or what capabilities do we give up?
- What does this decision make difficult to reverse?

### Alternatives considered

- Alternative 1 — why we did not choose it.
- Alternative 2 — why we did not choose it.

## References

- Links to relevant code, discussions, or external resources.
